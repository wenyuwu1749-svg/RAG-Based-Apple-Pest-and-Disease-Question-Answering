Index pages are not treated as articles; articles specific to pear, cherry and other crops are not used directly as apple evidence; reports on research projects are kept separately as background. Unknown dates stay unknown, and file modification times are not used as publication dates.
This is an offline review of relevance, structure and date scope; it is not a sentence-by-sentence check by plant protection experts and does not establish that the pesticide advice in all sources is still valid.
